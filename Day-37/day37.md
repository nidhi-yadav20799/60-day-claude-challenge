# Project Overview

Task Compass is an interactive management simulation that teaches how work flows inside a real technology company.

Instead of testing job titles, the application focuses on:

- Ownership
- Delegation
- Collaboration
- Workflow Thinking

Players complete interactive workplace scenarios through drag-and-drop gameplay while learning how different teams collaborate to solve business problems.
