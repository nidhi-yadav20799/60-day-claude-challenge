A quiet self-reflection experience

Cognitive Pattern Explorer

Wander through a few small scenarios and notice how your mind tends to move — toward logic, toward feeling, toward loops, toward action, or somewhere balanced between them. There are no right answers here, only patterns worth noticing.

Before you begin, choose a starting frame of mind

This shapes the tone of the scenarios you'll see — not your results. Pick whichever feels closer to how you actually feel right now.

This experience is educational and reflective only. It does not diagnose, assess, or treat any mental health condition, and it is not a substitute for professional support.
